# AI-flavor reduction

Use alongside a rewrite skill in `NARRAIVA_WRITE_V1`. Look for generic uplift, repeated rhetorical contrast, abstract emotional labels, over-balanced clauses, redundant explanation, and ornamental metaphors. Prefer concrete action, sensory specificity, subtext, varied rhythm, and vocabulary already supported by the passage.
