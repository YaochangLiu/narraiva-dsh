# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Narraiva DSH welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than people, accept constructive feedback, and protect authors’ private manuscripts and credentials. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and sexualized conduct are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and focused on community safety. This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
