# Narraiva trademark policy

The MIT license covers the source code, not the Narraiva name, word mark, logo, product identity, or other brand assets. Those marks are reserved by their respective owner.

You may truthfully describe an unmodified distribution as “Narraiva DSH” and say that a fork is “based on Narraiva DSH.” Modified distributions must not imply endorsement, official status, or origin from the Narraiva team. Forks that publish a product or hosted service should use a distinct name and visual identity unless written permission has been granted.

Community articles, compatibility statements, screenshots, and links may use the name nominatively when they do not create confusion. Open an issue before using Narraiva branding in a commercial product, domain, application icon, or package name.
